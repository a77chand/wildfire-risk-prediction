Wildfire ignition risk in British Columbia

The chance that each ~50 km grid cell sees a wildfire start in the next 7 days, from weather, the Canadian Fire Weather Index and 20 years of ignition records. Step back through every week since 2021, none of which the model was trained on, and compare it with the fires that actually started.

How well does it work?

ROC-AUC: how well risk separates cell-weeks with and without a fire (0.5 = coin flip). Timing: rank correlation between expected and actual weekly totals across the province. Climatology is the historical rate for that cell and month, a deliberately strong baseline.

Reading it honestly

  • It is best at when. The model tracks which weeks will be busy far better than the historical average does.
  • It is not much better at where, within a week. Watching the model's top cells covers about the same share of fires as watching the historically busiest cells. The crew slider shows this for any week.
  • 2026 is a true forward test. The model was trained on 2005–2018 and frozen before the 2026 season started. Each 2026 week is scored with only the data available that day; fire records are as they stand now.
  • Weather is NASA reanalysis at ~50 km and runs about 3 days behind, so "this week" is a nowcast, not a forecast. Lightning strikes and fuel condition are not inputs.

A research project, not a warning system. For real conditions use BC Wildfire Service.
